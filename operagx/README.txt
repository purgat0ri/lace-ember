LACE & EMBER for Opera GX

Dusk wine and crimson under Naamah's storm.

What's inside
- Three themes: Crimson, Ember and Gilt accents on dusk wine (bone in light mode)
- Naamah's Storm live wallpaper, and a still storm
- A full cursor set (pointer, link, text, resize, busy embers and more)
- Sidebar and toolbar icons: bone line-art with ember accents
- Two app icons: Naamah and the Sigil
- A splash screen with thunder and a bell
- Cinzel headers and Inter Tight body text in the GX interface
- YouTube, Google, DuckDuckGo and Reddit restyled in the palette
- Page shaders: Dusk, Embers, Embers · Storm (lightning), Embers · Bare
- Speed-dial hover effects: Kindle and Veil
- Bell-and-ember browser sounds, muted keys, and a storm soundtrack

How to install
1. Keep this folder somewhere permanent: Opera loads it from here.
2. Open opera://extensions and switch on Developer mode (top right).
3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose this folder.
4. Open GX Control (the GX button in the sidebar) → Mods, and pick the
   Lace & Ember options for theme, wallpaper, cursors, icons, splash screen, fonts,
   shaders, speed-dial effect, music and sounds.
5. App icon: opera://configure/mods/interface/app_icon → Lace & Ember, then
   restart Opera.
